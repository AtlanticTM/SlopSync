    Before you start#

    Read How it works. Clients that skip the mental model tend to fight the protocol: they build optimistic local state, then discover the hub disagrees with them.

    Keep the Dictionary open. Every term on this site has exactly one meaning, and the guides assume it.

    The one rule that catches everyone#

    Your shadow updates from the hub, never from your own request.

    You send an intent. The hub clamps it. The echo tells you what was actually applied. Render that. If you render what you asked for, your interface is lying, and on a machine that moves that is a safety defect.
